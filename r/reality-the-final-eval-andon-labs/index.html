Reality: The Final Eval — Andon Labs

#evals#observability#multi-agent#tools

Author: Latent.SpacePublished: 2026-06-04Reading time: ~87 min17455 wordsLanguage: enType: podcastCrawled: 2026-08-03

We talk with the VendingBench authors on evaling Claudes from Haiku to Mythos, and how they build leading, and lasting, frontier evals from scratch.

Lukas Petersson and Axel Backlund of Andon Labs discuss Vending-Bench, Project Vend, and Andon Market: dollar-denominated, long-horizon evals that give frontier models inventory, a wallet, tools, and time. Concrete case studies of harness design and failure modes — Claude calling the FBI over a $2 fee, long-context meltdown loops, multi-agent price cartels, election manipulation, and why Slack-as-observability works for tracing agent behavior that static benchmarks miss.
