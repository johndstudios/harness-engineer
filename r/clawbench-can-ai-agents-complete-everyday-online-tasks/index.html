ClawBench: Can AI Agents Complete Everyday Online Tasks?

https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.08523 · benchmark · huggingface.co · added 2026-08-02· by John Dilts

#benchmarks#web-agents

A browser-agent benchmark of 153 everyday web tasks across 144 live production sites in 15 categories, using a lightweight interception layer that captures and blocks only the final submission request so agents can be scored end-to-end on real websites without real-world side effects.
